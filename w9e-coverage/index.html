    Bugs found and fixed (byte-exact, with regression tests)

    1. The template date scan passed its end bound as a deadline (SusPatternsFileUploadTemplates.finditerWithin): python's _template_after_dates scans with pattern.finditer(content, pos, endpos) - the bound is an ENDPOS. The port called search(text, searchFrom, to.toLong()), feeding the bound as the DEADLINE, so every scan expired immediately and the date-anchored re-anchor of template_expression_matches was INERT (the corpus never exercises a date between two frames). Fixed with the endpos-bounded search overload; regression: the dated-template arms in SusPatternsW9eTailsTest.
